The Story Behind Earncial

Earncial was born from a simple but powerful observation: millions of people scroll through social media every day — liking posts, following accounts, watching videos — without earning a single penny for it. Meanwhile, brands and creators were spending enormous amounts trying to reach real audiences, often settling for fake engagement from bots.

In 2025, Earncial set out to fix this gap. The idea was straightforward — create a verified, transparent marketplace where real people earn money for the social media actions they already take naturally, and where advertisers get genuine, human engagement they can trust.

What started as a task marketplace quickly grew into something bigger. Today Earncial is building an entire ecosystem: a product marketplace where users can also buy and sell physical products, digital goods, and services — all within one trusted platform.

Earncial is fully registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) as Earncial Global, operating transparently and legally with a commitment to every user on the platform.
