1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work properly, remember your preferences, and provide information to the website owner about how users interact with the site.

Cookies do not contain personal information like your name or password on their own — they simply store a small identifier that helps the website recognise your browser on return visits.

There are two main types by lifespan:

  • Session cookies — temporary, deleted automatically when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies — stay on your device for a set period of time, or until you delete them manually

2. How We Use Cookies

Earncial uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Authentication — keeping you logged in securely across pages so you don't have to sign in on every click
  • Security — detecting fraudulent activity, preventing cross-site request forgery (CSRF), and protecting your account from unauthorised access
  • Preferences — remembering your settings such as dark mode / light mode preference
  • Platform functionality — enabling core features like task browsing, wallet management, and social handle management to work correctly
  • Analytics — understanding how users navigate the platform so we can improve the experience (optional)
  • Performance — helping pages load faster by caching certain content
✅ Earncial does not use cookies for advertising profiling, selling your data to third parties, or tracking you across other websites.

6. Your Cookie Controls

You have the right to control which optional cookies Earncial uses. Here are your options:

6.1 — Essential Cookies (Cannot Be Disabled)

Essential and security cookies are strictly necessary for the platform to function safely. Disabling them would prevent you from logging in, making payments, or using core features. These cannot be turned off while using Earncial.

6.2 — Optional Cookies (You Can Disable)

You may disable preference and analytics cookies at any time via:

  • Your browser settings (see Section 7 below)
  • Clearing your browser's cookies and site data
  • Using your browser's private / incognito mode — no persistent cookies are saved in incognito mode

6.3 — Effect of Disabling Cookies
