Step by Step

How to Start Earning

From registration to your first withdrawal — every step explained.

1
Create Your Earner Account
Go to Earncial Sign Up, fill in your details, and choose Earner as your account type. Registration is free and takes less than 2 minutes.
Choose your account type carefully — you cannot switch later
2
Activate Your Account (₦1,000 — One Time Only)
Pay the one-time activation fee of ₦1,000 via bank transfer to the details shown in your dashboard. Upload your payment receipt and your account is activated automatically once confirmed. You never pay this again.
One-time only — never charged again after activation
3
Link & Verify Your Social Media Handles
Go to Social Handles, tap Add Handle, choose a platform (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter, Telegram, and more), enter your username, copy the verification text shown, paste it in your profile bio on that platform, then submit for verification. Approval takes 1–6 hours.
Each platform is a separate verification — add as many as you want
4
Browse Available Tasks
Go to the Tasks page and see all live tasks available for your verified platforms. Each task shows the platform, task type, reward amount, and instructions. Pick tasks that match your verified handles.
More verified platforms = more tasks available to you
5
Complete the Task & Submit Proof
Tap Start Task → complete the action (follow, like, subscribe, comment, join, watch, share, etc.) on your verified account → take a clear screenshot showing your username and the completed action → return to Earncial → tap Submit Proof → upload the screenshot.
Screenshot must show your username clearly — blurry or cropped screenshots will be rejected
6
Get Approved & Earn
Our admin team reviews your submission within 1–6 hours. Once approved ✅, your earnings are instantly credited to your Earncial balance. You can track all your submissions and their status in My Tasks.
Do NOT undo the task action after submitting — this leads to rejection and possible ban
7
Withdraw to Your Bank Account 🎉
When your balance reaches ₦1,000 or more, go to Withdraw, select your bank account, enter the amount, review the fee breakdown (10% withdrawal fee), confirm — and your money arrives in your bank account. Save the receipt shown.
Minimum withdrawal: ₦1,000 · Withdrawal fee: 10% per transaction

Common Earner Questions

How much can I earn per task?
Each task has a fixed reward shown before you start. The amount varies by platform and task type. Complete more tasks and refer friends to multiply your earnings.
Why was my submission rejected?
The most common reasons: blurry or edited screenshot, your username is not visible, you used a different account than your verified handle, or you didn't follow the instructions exactly. Re-read the task, redo it carefully, and resubmit.
Can I do tasks on multiple platforms?
Yes! The more platforms you verify, the more tasks you can access. You can link Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter, Telegram, LinkedIn, and many more — all from the Social Handles page.
How fast is payment?
Once your withdrawal is confirmed, it processes within seconds and arrives in your bank account almost immediately. No waiting days or weeks.
Is Earncial legitimate? Can I really get paid?
Yes — Earncial is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) as Earncial Global, RC No. 9374009. You can verify this at search.cac.gov.ng. Thousands of earners have been paid directly to their bank accounts. See our Security FAQ →

Quick Answers

Common Advertiser Questions

Are the followers and engagement real?
Yes. Every earner's social media handle is manually verified before they can complete any task. Each task completion requires screenshot proof that is reviewed by our admin before being approved. No bots — only real people.
How much does a task cost?
Task cost = price per task × number of participants. The per-task price is automatically set by Earncial based on platform and task type. You see the full total before submitting — nothing is charged without your review.
Can I get a refund if I delete my task?
Yes. If you delete a task, you receive 95% of your remaining unused budget back to your wallet instantly. The 5% covers platform processing charges already applied at task creation.
Which platforms can I run tasks on?
All major platforms — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter/X, Telegram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Discord, Reddit, Twitch, Spotify, Snapchat, and more. New platforms are added regularly.
Why does Flutterwave charge more than I entered?
Flutterwave adds their own processing fee on top of your deposit amount. This fee goes directly to Flutterwave — not to Earncial. Your Earncial wallet is credited with exactly the amount you originally entered. Always pay the exact amount shown on Flutterwave.
