General

What Earncial is and how it works

1What is Earncial?

Earncial is a CAC-registered online platform that connects earners and advertisers worldwide.

Earners make money by completing simple social media tasks — following accounts, liking posts, subscribing to channels, commenting, joining groups, watching videos, sharing content, and more — across any platform.

Advertisers grow their social media presence by paying real, active users to engage with their content, gaining genuine followers, likes, comments, and subscribers.

💡 Earncial works on any device — phone, tablet, or computer — directly from your browser.
2Who can join Earncial?

Anyone can join Earncial. Two account types are available:

  • Earner Account — for individuals who want to earn money by completing social media tasks. Requires a one-time activation fee.
  • Advertiser Account — for businesses, brands, influencers, and creators who want to grow their social media by posting tasks for earners.
⚠️ You cannot switch account type after registration, so choose carefully.
3How does Earncial work overall?

For Earners: Register → Activate account (₦1,000 one-time) → Link your social handles → Get handles verified → Browse tasks → Complete tasks → Submit screenshot proof → Get paid once approved.

For Advertisers: Register → Deposit funds via Flutterwave → Post a task → Admin approves → Earners start completing it → Your social media grows.

All task completions are verified through proof submitted by earners and reviewed by Earncial's team before any payment is released.

4Which platforms and task types does Earncial support?

Earncial supports tasks across all social media platforms and task types, including but not limited to:

  • 📸 Instagram — Follow, Like, Comment, Story view, Reel watch, Share
  • 🎵 TikTok — Follow, Like, Comment, Watch, Share, Duet
  • ▶️ YouTube — Subscribe, Like, Comment, Watch, Share
  • 👤 Facebook — Page Like, Follow, Comment, Share, Join Group, React
  • 🐦 Twitter / X — Follow, Retweet, Like, Comment, Quote, Bookmark
  • ✈️ Telegram — Join Channel, Join Group, React, Forward, Share
  • 👻 Snapchat — Follow, View Story, Add Friend
  • 💼 LinkedIn — Follow, Connect, Like, Comment, Share, Endorse
  • 📌 Pinterest — Follow, Save Pin, Like, Board Follow
  • 👾 Discord — Join Server, React, Boost
  • 🔴 Reddit — Join Subreddit, Upvote, Comment, Award
  • 🎮 Twitch — Follow, Subscribe, Watch
  • 🎵 Spotify — Follow Artist, Save Playlist, Follow Podcast
  • 📱 And many more platforms and task types — new ones added regularly!
💡 Earncial continuously expands its platform and task type support. Check the Tasks page for the full up-to-date list.

Earner Guide

Activation, social handles, tasks, withdrawals

5How much is the activation fee and how do I pay it?

The activation fee is a one-time payment of ₦1,000. You only ever pay this once.

  1. Go to your Dashboard after registration
  2. Tap "Activate Account"
  3. Pay ₦1,000 via bank transfer to the account details shown
  4. Upload your payment proof / receipt
  5. Your account is activated automatically once payment is confirmed ✅
💡 One-time only — you will never be asked to pay this activation fee again.
6How do I link my social media handles to start doing tasks?

You must link and get approved for each platform separately before completing tasks on it:

  1. Go to the Social Handles page
  2. Tap "Add Handle"
  3. Select the platform (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, etc.)
  4. Enter your username for that platform
  5. Copy the verification text shown on screen
  6. Open that platform app → paste the verification text in your profile bio
  7. Return to Earncial → tap "Submit for Verification"
  8. ⏳ Wait for admin approval (1–6 hours)
  9. ✅ Once approved, you can complete tasks on that platform
⚠️ Each platform is a separate submission. Linking Instagram does not give you access to TikTok tasks — you must link each platform individually.
7How do I find and complete tasks to earn money?
  1. Go to the Tasks page — see all available live tasks
  2. Tap "View Task" to read the full instructions carefully
  3. Tap "Start Task" — you are taken to the task link
  4. Complete the required action (follow, like, subscribe, join, comment, watch, share, etc.)
  5. Come back to Earncial → tap "Submit Proof"
  6. Upload a clear screenshot showing the completed action
  7. ⏳ Admin reviews within 1–6 hours
  8. ✅ Approved → earnings credited to your balance automatically
💡 You can only complete tasks for platforms where your handle is approved.
8Why was my task submission rejected?

Common reasons for rejection:

  • Screenshot does not clearly show the completed action
  • Your username is not visible in the screenshot
  • You used a different account than your approved linked handle
  • Screenshot is blurry, cropped, or appears edited
  • You did not follow the exact task instructions
  • You undid the action after submitting (e.g. unfollowed after taking screenshot)

How to fix: Re-read the instructions carefully, redo the task on your exact linked account, take a fresh clear screenshot, and resubmit.

⚠️ The username in your screenshot must match your approved linked handle exactly.
9How do I withdraw my earnings?
  1. Make sure you have added your bank account in Bank Settings first
  2. Go to the Withdraw page
  3. Select your saved bank account
  4. Enter the amount to withdraw
  5. Review the breakdown: amount, platform fee, final amount you receive
  6. Click "Submit Withdrawal"
  7. Confirmation modal appears — double-check your details
  8. Click "Confirm" → processing begins
  9. ⏳ Wait a few seconds…
  10. 🧾 Withdrawal receipt appears — save it
  11. Close receipt → page scrolls to Withdrawal History
  12. Tap Refresh → status shows ✅ Completed
  13. 🎉 Check your bank — your money is there!
10How do I add my bank account for withdrawals?
  1. Go to Profile → Bank Settings
  2. Tap "Add Bank Account"
  3. Enter your bank name, account number, and account holder name
  4. Tap "Save" ✅
⚠️ The account name must match your registered Earncial name to avoid withdrawal delays or rejections.
11How does the referral system work?
  1. Go to the Referrals page
  2. Copy your unique referral link
  3. Share with friends via WhatsApp, social media, or anywhere
  4. When they sign up using your link and complete tasks → you earn a commission automatically 💰
  5. Track your referral earnings on the Referrals page
💡 The more active referrals you have, the more passive income you earn — with zero extra work!

Advertiser Guide

Deposits, posting tasks, managing campaigns

12How do I deposit funds into my advertiser wallet?
  1. Go to the Deposit page in your dashboard
  2. Enter the amount you want to add
  3. Select your payment method
  4. Click "Submit Deposit"
  5. You are redirected to Flutterwave — our secure payment provider
  6. Complete payment on Flutterwave
  7. ✅ Your wallet is credited automatically right after payment
⚠️ About the Flutterwave fee: Flutterwave adds their own processing fee on top. Example: You enter ₦10,000 on Earncial → Flutterwave shows ₦10,200. The extra ₦200 is Flutterwave's fee — NOT Earncial's. You must pay the exact amount shown on Flutterwave. Your wallet will be credited with your original ₦10,000.
13How do I post a task on Earncial?

Make sure your wallet has enough balance first, then go to New Task page:

  1. Select the Platform (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter, Telegram, and any other platform)
  2. Select the Task Type (Follow, Like, Subscribe, Comment, Join, Watch, Share, and any other type)
  3. Price per task is automatically set — no manual entry needed
  4. Add a Custom Description (optional but powerful — give earners specific instructions)
  5. Enter the Number of Participants needed (e.g. 100)
  6. Upload a Task Sample Image (optional — shows earners exactly where to go)
  7. Paste the Task URL — your exact profile/post/channel/group link. Double-check nothing is missing!
  8. Review the cost calculation: price × participants = total wallet deduction
  9. Click "Submit Task"

Our team reviews within minutes to a few hours. Once approved, earners start immediately! 🚀

💡 Manage all your tasks from the Manage Tasks page.
14Can I increase participants after my task is already live?

Yes! You can increase participants at any time, even while the task is running.

Two ways to do it:

  • Go to Manage Tasks → find your task → tap the ➕ plus icon button
  • Or: Manage Tasks → "View Task Details" → "Increase Participants"

Enter the number of additional participants (minimum 10), review the cost, confirm. Participants are added instantly and the cost is deducted from your wallet immediately.

🎉 No re-approval needed! Your task stays live and earners continue completing it right away.
15Can I pause or stop a running task?

Yes. Go to Manage Tasks and use the task controls:

  • ⏸️ Pause: Tap the pause button — task is immediately hidden from earners. No new completions until you resume.
  • ▶️ Resume: Tap the resume button — task goes live again instantly and earners continue.
💡 Pausing does not lose your budget or delete the task. It simply stops new completions temporarily.
16Can I delete a task? Will I get my budget back?

Yes, you can delete any task. Go to Manage Tasks → find the task → tap the 🗑️ Delete button.

Refund policy: You receive 95% of your remaining unused budget back to your wallet instantly.

Example:

  • Total task budget: ₦10,000
  • Already spent on earners: ₦5,000
  • Remaining unused: ₦5,000
  • Refund = 95% of ₦5,000 = ₦4,750 back to your wallet
⚠️ 100% refund is not possible because platform processing charges were already applied when the task was created. The 5% is non-recoverable.
17How do I view my task analytics and performance?

Go to the Analytics page in your advertiser dashboard to see:

  • 📈 Task completion rates and trends over time
  • 💰 Spending breakdown by task and platform
  • 👥 Participant progress per task
  • 📅 Weekly, monthly, and yearly performance views
  • 🏆 Best performing tasks and platforms
💡 Use analytics to discover which platforms and task types give you the best return, then optimise your budget accordingly.

Technical

Bugs, login issues, and app problems

18I can't log in to my Earncial account. What do I do?
  1. Double-check your email and password are correct
  2. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
  3. Check your inbox and spam/junk folder for the reset link
  4. Set a new password and try logging in again

Still locked out? Email [email protected] with your registered email and description of the issue.

19The app is slow or not loading. How do I fix it?
  1. Refresh the page
  2. Check your internet — switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data
  3. Clear browser cache and cookies
  4. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  5. On mobile: close the app fully and reopen it

If the issue continues for 30+ minutes, submit a support ticket.

20I found a bug or error on the platform. How do I report it?

Submit a support ticket with:

  • Which page you were on
  • What you clicked or did
  • The exact error message shown
  • Your device and browser
  • A screenshot if possible 📸
💡 All bugs are investigated promptly. Your report helps improve the experience for every user on Earncial.

Pricing & Fees

Costs, charges, and refund policy

21What fees do earners pay on Earncial?
  • Activation fee: ₦1,000 one-time only — never paid again after activation
  • Withdrawal fee: A small platform processing charge shown transparently before you confirm — you see the exact amount you'll receive before clicking confirm
💡 No hidden fees for earners. Task completion itself is always free.
22How is the cost of a task calculated for advertisers?

Task cost = Price per task × Number of participants = Total wallet deduction

  • The price per task is automatically set by Earncial based on the platform and task type
  • The total is shown clearly before you submit — nothing is deducted without your review
💡 You see the full cost calculation on the New Task page before clicking Submit.
23Why does Flutterwave charge more than what I entered on Earncial?

Flutterwave is our payment provider and they add their own processing fee. This fee belongs to Flutterwave — not Earncial.

  • You enter ₦10,000 on Earncial
  • On Flutterwave you see ₦10,200
  • ₦200 = Flutterwave's fee
  • You pay ₦10,200 on Flutterwave
  • Your Earncial wallet gets ₦10,000 ✅
⚠️ Always pay the exact amount shown on Flutterwave — not what you entered on Earncial.
24What is the advertiser refund policy when deleting a task?

When you delete a task you receive 95% of your remaining unused budget back to your wallet instantly.

  • The 5% covers platform processing charges already applied at task creation
  • Refund is returned to your Earncial wallet instantly upon deletion
💡 Deposited funds not yet spent on any task remain in your wallet and do not expire.
