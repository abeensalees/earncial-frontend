Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining Earncial.

Is Earncial legitimate? Can I really get paid?
Yes — Earncial Global is officially registered with Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission, RC No. 9374009. You can verify this yourself at search.cac.gov.ng. Thousands of earners have been paid directly to their bank accounts since we launched in 2025.
How much does it cost to join as an earner?
Registration is free. There is a one-time activation fee of ₦1,000 which you pay only once — never again. After that, you can earn indefinitely. There is a 10% fee on each withdrawal, and a minimum withdrawal of ₦1,000.
How fast do I receive my withdrawal?
Withdrawals are processed and typically arrive in your bank account within minutes to a few hours. No waiting days. Your bank account must match the name on your Earncial profile.
Are the followers advertisers get real people?
Yes. Every earner's social media handle is manually verified before they can complete any task. Each submission requires screenshot proof reviewed by our admin. Advertisers receive real, human engagement — not bots.
Which social media platforms does Earncial support?
Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter/X, Telegram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Discord, Reddit, Spotify, Twitch, Snapchat — and more platforms are added regularly. The more platforms you verify, the more tasks you can access.
Can I use Earncial from outside Nigeria?
Earncial is accessible globally. However, bank withdrawals currently process to Nigerian bank accounts. International payment options and expanded withdrawal methods are part of our roadmap.
