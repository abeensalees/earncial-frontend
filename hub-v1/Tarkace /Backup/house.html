Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining Earncial.

Is Earncial legitimate? Can I really get paid?
Yes — Earncial Global is officially registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission, RC No. 9374009. You can verify this yourself at search.cac.gov.ng. Thousands of earners have been paid directly to their bank accounts since we launched in 2025.
How much does it cost to join as an earner?
Registration is free. There is a one-time activation fee of ₦1,000 — paid only once, never again. After activation, there is a 10% fee on each withdrawal and a minimum withdrawal of ₦1,000.
How fast do I receive my withdrawal?
Withdrawals typically arrive in your bank account within minutes to a few hours. Your bank account name must match the name on your Earncial profile.
Are the followers and likes advertisers get real people?
Yes. Every earner's social media handle is manually verified before they can complete any task. Each submission requires screenshot proof reviewed by our admin. Advertisers receive genuine human engagement — no bots.
Which countries can use Earncial?
Earncial is accessible globally. However, bank withdrawals currently process to Nigerian bank accounts. International payout options are part of our roadmap. Anyone worldwide can advertise on the platform.
What happens if my task is rejected?
If a task submission is rejected by admin (e.g. unclear screenshot), you can re-submit with corrected proof. Repeated violations of task rules (fake screenshots, undoing tasks) result in permanent account termination per our Terms of Service.
