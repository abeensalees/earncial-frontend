Connecting advertisers with earners for mutual benefit in Nigeria's digital economy

Advertisers post tasks (follows, likes, comments), and earners complete them for payment. We provide complete transparency in pricing and ensure fair earnings for everyone. Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Earncial

Is Earncial a legitimate platform?

Yes! Earncial is a verified platform with over 25,000 active Nigerian users. We have processed over ₦50 million in payments. Check our social media for daily payment proofs and user testimonials.

Must I refer people to withdraw money?

Yes, this is important: Earners must refer at least ONE person to unlock withdrawals. This policy ensures community growth. Start referring early to avoid delays when you reach the ₦1,000 minimum withdrawal.

How do withdrawals work?

Withdrawals are processed every Friday (8:00 AM – 6:00 PM WAT). Minimum withdrawal is ₦1,000. Submit your request anytime, and it will be handled during the next Friday payout window. Important: Ensure your bank details are correct as withdrawals to wrong accounts cannot be reversed.

How much can I earn daily?

Active users typically earn ₦2,000 - ₦5,000 daily from tasks alone. With referrals and bonuses, some top earners make ₦10,000+ daily. Your earnings depend on your activity level - Earncial isn't for the lazy!

Are there any hidden fees?

No hidden fees! We believe in complete transparency. Task prices are clearly displayed, and you see exactly what you'll earn. The only requirement is that earners must refer at least one person to unlock withdrawals.

How do I contact support?

Use the Support Page or email support@earncial.com. Always include your full name and clear description for faster help. Average response time: 2-6 hours during business days. We also have active WhatsApp and Telegram support groups.
