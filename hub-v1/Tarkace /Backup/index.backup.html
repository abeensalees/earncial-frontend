What is Earncial?

Connecting advertisers with earners for mutual benefit

At Earncial, we understand the importance of connecting Advertisers and Earners in today's digital landscape. Our platform empowers advertisers to boost their social media visibility by creating tasks for earners to complete.

In return, Earners are compensated for completing these straightforward activities on social media platforms. Our intuitive interface and secure payment system streamline the process for advertisers to post jobs and for earners to fulfill them and receive payment.

We offer progress analytics and prompt, helpful customer support to ensure our clients are pleased with their experience. Join the Earncial community today and start growing your online presence or earning money through simple tasks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Earncial

What is Earncial?

Earncial is an online earning and advertising platform that connects advertisers with real people (earners). Advertisers post tasks (follows, likes, comments, joins, etc.), and earners complete them and get paid.

How do I register?

Click the "Sign Up" button anywhere on the website, fill in your details (full name, username, email, phone, password), and choose your role (Advertiser or Earner). Choose carefully as you can't switch roles later.

How do withdrawals work?

Withdrawals are processed every Friday, from 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM (WAT). Submit your request anytime, and it will be handled during the next Friday payout window. Minimum withdrawal is ₦1,000.

Can I refer someone?

Yes! Earncial has a referral program. Invite friends using your referral link. When they register as earners, you get 2% lifetime reward from every withdrawal they make. If they register as advertisers, you get 3% lifetime reward from every deposit they make.

How do I post a task as an advertiser?

Go to your dashboard → Post New Task → Choose a Category → Choose the task type → Paste a valid link → Enter participants amount → Review the automatic calculation. Prices per task are pre-set by Earncial for fairness.

How do I contact support?

Use the Support Page or official channels listed in the footer. Always include your full name, email (if relevant), and a clear description for faster help.
