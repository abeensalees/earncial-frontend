⚠️ Important Guidelines - Please Read!

Screenshot Verification: All task completions require a clear screenshot as proof. Without a valid screenshot showing the completed action (follow, like, comment, etc.), submissions will be automatically rejected.

Expected Completion Rate: Due to factors like blocked accounts, unavailable content, or user restrictions, expect 85-95% completion rate, not 100%. Some users may not be able to complete tasks due to platform limitations.

Account Must Be Public: For tasks like follows, likes, or views, your account MUST be public. Private accounts will result in zero completions as users cannot access your content.

Platform Restrictions: Some users may have already followed you, blocked you, or reached their daily action limits. This is normal and affects completion rates. Plan accordingly when setting participant numbers.

Review Process: All tasks undergo admin review before going live (typically within 24 hours). Tasks promoting illegal content, hate speech, or violating platform policies will be rejected with full refund.

Sample Image (Recommended): Uploading a sample screenshot helps users understand exactly what to do, increasing completion rates by up to 30%. Show the exact post, profile, or content to engage with.
