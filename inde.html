Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining Earncial.

Is Earncial legitimate? Can I really get paid?
Yes — Earncial Global is officially registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission, RC No. 9374009. Verify yourself at search.cac.gov.ng. Thousands of earners have been paid to their bank accounts since 2025.
How much does it cost to join as an earner?
Registration is completely free. There is a one-time activation fee of ₦1,000 — paid only once, never again. After that, a 10% fee applies on each withdrawal with a minimum withdrawal of ₦1,000.
How fast do I receive my withdrawal?
Withdrawals are processed automatically and credited instantly to your bank account. There are no manual delays — the moment your withdrawal request is confirmed, payment is sent out automatically by our system. Your bank account name must match your Earncial profile name exactly.
Is the activation fee payment automatic and safe?
Yes. The ₦1,000 activation fee is processed automatically and instantly through Flutterwave — a trusted, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. You pay once, your account is activated automatically within seconds, and you will never be charged again.
Are the followers and likes real people?
Yes. Every earner's social handle is manually verified before completing any task. Each submission requires screenshot proof reviewed by admin. Advertisers receive genuine human engagement — zero bots, guaranteed.
Can I earn up to ₦3,000 per day?
Yes, it's achievable — but it depends on the number of social handles you verify, the platforms available, and how consistently you complete tasks. Earners with multiple verified accounts across 5+ platforms can hit this target.
Which countries can use Earncial?
Earncial is accessible globally. Bank withdrawals currently process to Nigerian bank accounts. International payout options are part of our roadmap.
