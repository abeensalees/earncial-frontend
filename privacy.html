1. Overview

Welcome to Earncial ("we", "us", or "our"). Earncial is operated by Earncial Global (CAC RC No. 9374009), a registered business.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how we protect it, and what rights you have over your data when you use the Earncial platform, website, or any related services (collectively, the "Service").

By creating an account or using any part of the Earncial Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Service.

💡 This policy applies to all users of Earncial — both Earners (individuals completing tasks) and Advertisers (individuals or businesses posting tasks).

Who is the Data Controller?

Earncial Global is the data controller responsible for your personal information collected through the Earncial platform. For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at support@earncial.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information collected automatically, and information from third parties.

2.1 — Information You Provide

Data Type What We Collect When Collected
Account Information Full name, username, email address, password (hashed), phone number, gender, country Registration
Bank Account Details Bank name, account number, account holder name When you add a bank account (earners)
Social Media Handles Platform name and your username on that platform When you link a handle for task verification
Task Submissions Screenshot images, task completion records When you submit proof for a task
Payment Information Deposit amounts, transaction references (card details processed by Flutterwave — not stored by us) Deposits and withdrawals
Support Communications Messages and files you send to our support team When you contact support
Referral Information Referral codes used at sign-up Registration

2.2 — Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type and version
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent, clicks, and navigation patterns
  • Log Data: IP address, access timestamps, error logs
  • Cookies & Tracking: Session cookies, preference cookies (see Section 6)

2.3 — Information from Third Parties

  • Flutterwave: Payment transaction status and references (not your full card details)
  • Social Media Platforms: We do not access your social media accounts directly — we only record the username you provide and verify it manually
⚠️ We never store full card numbers, CVV codes, or PINs. All payment card data is handled exclusively by Flutterwave's PCI-DSS certified infrastructure.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

3.1 — Providing the Service

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Verifying your social media handles for task eligibility
  • Displaying available tasks and matching earners to advertisers
  • Processing task submissions and approvals
  • Processing deposits and withdrawals
  • Calculating and crediting earnings to your balance
  • Managing referral commissions

3.2 — Account & Security

  • Authenticating your identity when you log in
  • Sending password reset emails and security alerts
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and fake accounts
  • Investigating and resolving disputes between earners and advertisers
  • Verifying the authenticity of task submission screenshots

3.3 — Communication

  • Sending transaction receipts and withdrawal confirmations
  • Responding to your support requests
  • Sending platform updates, policy changes, and important notices
  • Sending optional promotional emails (you may unsubscribe at any time)

3.4 — Platform Improvement

  • Analysing usage patterns to improve platform features and performance
  • Generating aggregated, anonymised analytics (not linked to you personally)
  • Conducting internal research and quality assurance

3.5 — Legal Compliance

  • Complying with applicable laws and regulations
  • Responding to lawful requests from regulatory authorities
  • Maintaining records required for business registration (CAC) purposes
💡 We process your data based on the following legal grounds: performance of a contract (to provide the Service), legitimate interests (fraud prevention, security), legal obligation (compliance), and consent (marketing emails).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to anyone. We share your data only in the limited circumstances described below.

4.1 — Service Providers

ProviderPurposeData Shared
Flutterwave Payment processing for deposits Name, email, amount, transaction reference
Hosting / Cloud Infrastructure Storing platform data securely Account and transaction data (encrypted)
Email Service Provider Sending transactional and support emails Email address, name

All service providers are contractually required to process your data only for the specified purpose and to maintain appropriate security standards.

4.2 — Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or in response to a verified request from a regulatory authority or law enforcement agency.

4.3 — Business Transfers

If Earncial is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

4.4 — With Your Consent

We may share your information in any other way we describe to you at the time of collection, or with your explicit consent.

✅ We never sell your personal data. We never share your data with advertisers on the platform for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

5. Data Storage & Security

5.1 — How We Store Your Data

  • Your data is stored on secure, encrypted servers
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms — we never store plaintext passwords
  • Bank account details are encrypted at rest
  • Payment card data is never stored on Earncial servers — it is handled entirely by Flutterwave

5.2 — Security Measures

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your device and our servers
  • Access controls ensuring only authorised team members can access sensitive data
  • Regular security reviews and monitoring for suspicious activity
  • Account lockout protection against brute-force login attempts

5.3 — How Long We Keep Your Data

Data TypeRetention Period
Account informationFor as long as your account is active + 2 years after deletion
Transaction records7 years (legal/tax requirement)
Task submission screenshots90 days after approval/rejection
Support communications3 years
Log files12 months
Marketing preferencesUntil you unsubscribe or delete your account
⚠️ While we implement strong security measures, no system is 100% impenetrable. If you suspect your account has been compromised, change your password immediately and contact support@earncial.com.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve the Earncial platform.

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie TypePurposeCan Be Disabled?
Essential / Session Keeping you logged in, maintaining your session securely No — required for the platform to work
Preference Remembering your dark/light mode setting and language preference Yes
Analytics Understanding how users navigate the platform (aggregated, anonymous) Yes

You can manage or disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

9. Children's Privacy

Earncial is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age.

By creating an account on Earncial, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a person under 18, we will delete that data immediately and close the associated account.

⚠️ If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 18 has created an Earncial account, please contact us immediately at support@earncial.com and we will take swift action to remove the account and all associated data.
