2. About Our Platform

Earncial operates as a multi-service digital platform comprising the following services:

2.1 — Task Marketplace (Live)

A marketplace that connects Earners and Advertisers for the completion of social media engagement tasks. Earncial acts as an intermediary — facilitating connections, verifying task completions, and processing payments — but is not a party to the agreement between individual earners and advertisers.

  • Earners complete social media tasks (follow, like, subscribe, comment, join, watch, share, etc.) in exchange for monetary compensation
  • Advertisers post tasks and pay for genuine social media engagement to grow their online presence
  • Earncial verifies task completion through screenshot review before releasing payment

  • Services — freelance and professional services offered by users
💡 Earncial is a marketplace intermediary. We connect buyers and sellers and earners and advertisers, but we are not the seller of goods nor the employer of earners. All transactions are between independent users of the platform.

Each user is permitted to hold one account only. Creating multiple accounts — whether as an earner, an advertiser, or across both types — is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate permanent banning of all associated accounts and forfeiture of all balances.

You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and for all activity that occurs under your account. You agree to:

You must provide truthful and accurate information at registration and keep it updated. Providing false information — including a false name, age, bank account, or social media handles — may result in immediate account termination and forfeiture of earnings.

4. Earner Terms

4.1 — Activation

Before completing any tasks, earners must pay a one-time, non-refundable activation fee of ₦1,000. This fee covers account setup, verification processing, and platform access. It is charged once and never again.

4.2 — Social Media Handle Verification

Earners must link and verify their social media handles before completing tasks on any platform. The verification process requires:

  1. Submitting your username for the target platform
  2. Placing a Earncial-provided verification text in your profile bio
  3. Submitting for admin review and approval

Each platform must be verified separately. Earncial may revoke handle approval at any time if the handle is found to violate these Terms.

4.3 — Completing Tasks

When completing tasks, earners agree to:

  • Read and follow task instructions carefully and exactly
  • Complete the task using only your verified, linked account
  • Submit a clear, unedited screenshot clearly showing your username and the completed action
  • Maintain the completed action — you must not undo it (e.g. unfollow, unlike, leave group) after submission
  • Not submit a task you have previously completed

4.4 — Earnings & Withdrawals

  • Earnings are credited to your Earncial balance only after task approval by Earncial admin
  • Minimum withdrawal amount: ₦1,000
  • A 10% fee is deducted from every withdrawal
  • Withdrawals are processed to your registered bank account only
  • The bank account name must match your registered Earncial name
💡 The withdrawal fee breakdown is shown transparently before you confirm — you always know exactly how much you will receive.

4.5 — Nature of Relationship

Earners on Earncial are independent users, not employees, contractors, or agents of Earncial. Completing tasks does not create an employment relationship between you and Earncial or between you and the advertiser who posted the task.

5. Advertiser Terms

5.1 — Wallet & Deposits

  • Advertisers must fund their Earncial wallet before posting tasks
  • Minimum deposit amount: ₦1,000
  • Deposits are processed via Flutterwave. Flutterwave adds its own processing fee — the amount shown on Flutterwave may be higher than the amount entered on Earncial. You must pay the exact amount shown on Flutterwave
  • Wallet funds do not expire and may be used for any tasks posted on the platform

5.2 — Posting Tasks

By posting a task on Earncial, the advertiser warrants that:

  • They own or have the right to promote the social media account, content, or product in the task
  • The task URL is accurate, complete, and leads directly to the correct destination
  • The task does not violate the terms of any social media platform it targets
  • The task does not promote illegal, harmful, deceptive, or offensive content
  • They have sufficient wallet balance to cover the full task cost before submitting

Tasks are subject to admin review before going live. Earncial reserves the right to reject any task without explanation.

5.3 — Task Management

  • Pause / Resume: Tasks may be paused and resumed at any time at no cost
  • Increase Participants: Additional participants (minimum 10) may be added to a live task at any time — the cost is deducted instantly and no re-approval is required
  • Delete Task: Advertisers may delete a task and receive a 95% refund of the remaining unused budget to their wallet. The 5% is non-refundable and covers platform processing charges

5.4 — Approval & Rejection of Submissions

Earncial's admin team reviews all earner task submissions. Advertisers do not manually approve or reject individual submissions — this is managed by Earncial to ensure fairness and prevent abuse. Advertisers agree to accept Earncial's review decisions on task submissions.

5.5 — Prohibited Tasks

Advertisers may not post tasks that:

  • Promote illegal products, services, or activities
  • Contain sexually explicit, violent, or hateful content
  • Involve phishing, scams, or deceptive schemes
  • Violate the intellectual property rights of third parties
  • Direct earners to download malicious software or visit harmful websites
  • Request earners to share personal financial or sensitive information
🚫 Posting prohibited tasks will result in immediate account suspension, forfeiture of wallet balance, and may be reported to relevant authorities.
